The game combines ranch management, farming, animal care, building, crafting, cooking, fishing, exploration, and casual outdoor activities.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Rancher: A new life**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for managing a ranch, caring for animals, exploring the countryside, completing construction projects, and enjoying the game's farming and leisure systems with fewer resource and stamina limitations.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce repetitive resource management, experiment with ranch layouts, accelerate farming and construction, or freely explore the game's animals, cooking, fishing, gardening, vehicles, and leisure activities.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options while traveling around the ranch, forest paths, and nearby locations on foot, by ATV, horseback, or in restored vehicles. Unlimited stamina is useful for extended exploration, gathering, mowing, and ranch work.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate inventory options before moving materials or purchasing supplies. Unlimited items are intended for building materials, food ingredients, crops, animal products, tools, and workshop resources. Avoid changing item quantities while transferring objects between containers, vehicles, and the player inventory.

### World & Environment

Money and fast construction options support ranch renovation, furniture purchases, workshop expansion, vehicle restoration, greenhouse projects, animal facilities, and decorative improvements. Crop and animal supply options can simplify gardening, greenhouse management, feeding, watering, breeding, and product collection.

### Combat & Weapons

Rancher: A New Life focuses on rural simulation rather than traditional combat. The relevant offensive activity is the shooting range, where improved accuracy can help with target-based challenges. Use combat-related options only while participating in shooting activities.
